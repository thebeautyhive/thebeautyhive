Before You Book

Quick answers, no surprises.

How long does the appointment take?
Plan for about 2 hours 45 minutes for the Short/Medium combo and 3 hours 15 minutes for Long/Extra-Long. Your appointment is private and one-on-one — no rushing, no overlap with other guests.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
A $25 deposit secures your appointment. Reschedule or cancel with at least 48 hours' notice and your deposit transfers to a future visit — no questions asked. Within 48 hours, the deposit is forfeited so we can offer the spot to another guest.
Is this a gift she can use for herself, or do we come together?
Both work. You can book the appointment for Mom directly under her name, or message @isssbeenails on Instagram for a digital gift card she can redeem on her own schedule.
What's the difference between Acrylic, Gel-X, and Builder Gel?
Acrylic is the most durable for long lengths. Gel-X uses pre-shaped soft-gel tips for a lighter, more natural feel. Builder Gel is the gentlest on natural nails and ideal for adding strength without extending much length. Brenda will help you pick the right one when you arrive.
What's included in the price?
Everything in the hero list: full hands service in your chosen system (Acrylic, Gel-X, or Builder Gel), full feet service, paraffin wax foot treatment, custom nail art, botanical hand massage, and a take-home luxury cuticle care kit. The only extras are optional add-ons (e.g. additional art tiers) which you'll see during booking.
