Good to Know

A few quiet details.

Who qualifies for the Welcome Experience?

The Welcome Experience is offered exclusively to first-time guests of Beauty Hive Studio booking a Gel-X or Builder Gel service. One per guest.

Do I need to redeem a code at checkout?

No code is required. When you book a first-time Gel-X or Builder Gel appointment through the form above, the complimentary ritual is added to your visit automatically.

Can I gift this to a friend?

The Welcome Experience is reserved for the first-time guest at their own appointment. If you'd like to send a friend, simply share this page — they can claim their spot the same way.

How long does the appointment take?

Plan for the time of your booked Gel-X or Builder Gel service. The Welcome Experience is woven into your appointment so you can fully relax — it does not require additional time.

Where is the studio located?

Beauty Hive Studio is located at 6244 El Cajon Blvd, Suite 21, San Diego, CA 92115 — in the College Area near SDSU. Parking is available on site.

What if I need to reschedule?

We ask for at least 48 hours' notice for any reschedule or cancellation so your spot can be offered to another guest. With proper notice, your $25 deposit is fully transferable.
