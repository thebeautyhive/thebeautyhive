Good to Know

A few quiet details.

What is the Signature Set?

A custom full-set appointment built around your base service, shape, length, and art direction. Signature sets start at $135 before tip.

Do I need to pick a specific service?

Book the full set that matches your nails and length. If you are unsure, DM Brenda your inspo and she will point you to the right option.

Can I gift this to a friend?

Yes. Share the page with them so they can book their own appointment or DM Brenda with inspo first.

How long does the appointment take?

Plan for the time listed on the service you book. Detailed art can require a different tier, so send inspo if your design is complex.

Where is the studio located?

Beauty Hive Studio is located at 6244 El Cajon Blvd, Suite 21, San Diego, CA 92115 — in the College Area of San Diego. Parking is available on site.

What if I need to reschedule?

We ask for at least 48 hours' notice for any reschedule or cancellation so your spot can be offered to another guest. With proper notice, your $25 deposit is fully transferable.
