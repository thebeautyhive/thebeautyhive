Module 1 · Lesson 1

Offer Ladder

Two simple paths, with design protected as the premium upsell.

The goal is not to advertise every service. The front-end should be easy to understand, high enough to protect AOV, and simple enough for a new client to DM without knowing every length, shape, and art tier.

Learn

The two front-end offers that make the booking path easier.

Why it matters

Clients should not need to decode the full service menu before messaging.

Do

Route full sets to SET, natural nail support to GROW, and detailed inspo to DESIGN.

Offer 1

The Grow-Out Reset

$85+ starting point

DM GROW Builder gel Natural nail care

For clients with broken, thin, picked, short, or damaged nails who want cute nails while their real nails grow.

Positioning: "Cute nails while your real nails grow." Keep the content educational: prep, structure, protection, retention, and healthy grow-out.

Offer 2

Send The Inspo

$120+ starting point

DM SET Acrylic or Gel-X Length + shape

For clients who want length, shape, and a polished first full set without having to understand every booking-menu option first.

Positioning: "Your first full set, shaped and designed for your hands." Simple or classic finish can be included. Detailed custom art is quoted from inspo.

Do not make "free custom design" the broad offer. Design is the high-ticket ladder: simple finish first, detailed art quoted from inspo, heavy design priced higher.

Module 3 · Lessons 1-4

30-Day Calendar

The first month of content.

Treat each week like a course lesson. Week 1 is open because it is the first assignment. The rest stay closed until Brenda gets there.

How Brenda should talk about price.

Do not lead with an apology or a price-increase announcement. The content should make the value obvious: prep, structure, retention, cleanliness, design planning, and the private one-on-one experience.

  • Say: "Structured sets start at..." instead of "I had to raise my prices."
  • Say: "Detailed art is quoted from inspo because every set takes different time." instead of defending the cost.
  • Say: "I want you booked for the right service." instead of over-explaining the menu.
  • Keep the tone calm, specific, and expert. No apologizing for premium work.

Module 4 · Lesson 1

DM Funnel

Keep the keywords simple.

The first conversation should qualify the client, reduce decision friction, and send the right booking path.

  • DM GROW: builder gel / natural nail recovery path.

Module 4 · Lesson 2

Weekly Workflow

How Brenda should use this every week.

Learn

How to turn this plan into a repeatable weekly habit.

Why it matters

The account grows from repeated proof and clear replies, not from one big planning session.

Do

Pick ideas, label the asset job, post, then track which DMs become real appointments.

1. Pick 10 ideas

Choose from the pillars: grow-out, full set, design pricing, private suite, proof, and availability.

2. Mark each idea

Label each as reuse, shoot, or generate. Reuse old winners first, but assign every reused Reel a new job: proof, education, personality, or booking.

3. Post and track

Track DMs, qualified leads, booked appointments, and which hooks create real booking conversations.

Final Lesson

Approval Checklist

  • Final names and starting prices for GROW and SET offers.

  • Whether SET should be acrylic-first, Gel-X-first, or Brenda-guided based on client goals.
  • How clients should book art inside Acuity: add-on, separate row, or appointment note.
  • DM flow copy and follow-up timing.
  • Where non-booked leads should be tracked for follow-up.
