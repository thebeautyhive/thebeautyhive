Reuse Rule

Remix old winners with a new job.

ChangeFirst frame, overlay, caption angle, and CTA.
KeepThe strongest nail proof or Brenda personality moment.
AvoidSame video, same caption, no new booking direction.

Every Reused Reel Needs One Job

Before editing, label the asset as proof, education, personality, or booking. Then cut the Reel around that one job so it feels intentional, not recycled.

Reuse Checklist

  1. Open with the best 1-2 seconds: finished nails, Brenda's face, or the strongest process detail.
  2. Add one short overlay that tells the viewer why this set matters.
  3. Trim repeated setup. Reused Reels should usually be tighter than the original.
  4. Use one CTA only: SET, GROW, DESIGN, BOOK, or link in bio.
  5. If the asset is only a strong still, make it a carousel/static or send it to motion adaptation instead of forcing a Reel.

Current Video

First Set / Send The Inspo

GoalGet a potential new client to DM SET with nail inspo or click the booking link.
HookSan Diego, send the nail inspo you have been saving.
CTADM SET.

Finished Video Should Look Like

A polished 8-14 second Reel that opens with the finished nails immediately. The first frame should be a tight, bright close-up of the full set, not Brenda talking and not a title screen. The viewer should understand one thing: send the inspo you have saved, and Brenda will help you choose the right set.

Shot List

  1. Finished set close-up, nails filling most of the frame.
  2. Slow hand turn so the shape and shine catch the light.
  3. Macro shot of the best art detail, held steady.
  4. Brenda doing one process step: top coat, detail paint, chrome, charm, or file.
  5. Client hand resting on a clean surface for the final reveal.
  6. Quick phone shot of an inspo photo or Brenda looking at inspo.
  7. Optional Brenda clip: "Send me your inspo and I will help you choose what to book."

Editing Direction

  • Start with the prettiest finished-nail shot.
  • Cut every 1-2 seconds.
  • Keep text minimal: "Send the nail inspo you have been saving."
  • End text: "DM SET."
  • Do not explain the full strategy or offer terms in this video.

Production Copy

On-Screen Text
  • San Diego, send the nail inspo you have been saving.
  • Bring the inspo you have been saving.
  • DM SET to book with Brenda.
Caption

San Diego, if you have been saving nail inspo, send it over. DM SET and I will help you figure out what to book.

Optional VO

Send me the inspo you have been saving and I will help you figure out the right full set.

Builder Gel Growth Proof

HookCute nails, but make them your real nails.
Reuse AssetBuilder/natural nail clip or DR7kwtkj4J8.
CTADM GROW.

Finished Video Should Look Like

A 12-18 second educational proof Reel. It should feel calm, clean, and credible, not salesy. The viewer should understand that builder gel is for someone who wants pretty nails while protecting or growing their natural nails. The video should show nail health, prep, structure, and the final result in that order.

Shot List

  1. Before shot of natural nails or grow-out, filmed straight-on with good light.
  2. Close-up of cuticle prep or gentle cleaning.
  3. Shot of Brenda shaping the nail evenly.
  4. Builder gel application from the side so the viewer can see structure.
  5. Light reflection shot showing the apex and smoothness of the nail.
  6. Finished set close-up, natural-looking and clean.
  7. Final hand turn showing the nails from both top and side angles.
  8. Optional talking-head line: "Builder gel is for my clients who want cute nails and want to grow their real nails underneath."

Editing Direction

  • Open with a quick before/after split or before-to-after cut.
  • Overlay text: "Cute nails, but make them your real nails."
  • Add 2-3 short labels only: prep, structure, growth support.
  • End text: "DM GROW."

Day 4

Custom Set Cost

HookWhat does a custom set cost?
FormatCarousel or a simple 12-15 second Reel.
CTASend inspo for the right appointment.

Finished Video Should Look Like

A clean, helpful explainer that removes confusion before someone DMs. It should not feel like a hard price list or discount post. It should show examples of simple, classic, and more detailed art so a client understands why pricing depends on length, service, and design complexity.

Shot List

  1. Shot of a simple clean set, no heavy art.
  2. Shot of a classic-art set with one or two design details.
  3. Shot of a more detailed or signature art set.
  4. Screen recording or over-the-shoulder shot of Brenda looking at inspo on a phone.
  5. Brenda pointing to the parts of the inspo that affect price: length, charms, French, 3D, chrome, hand-painted detail.
  6. Finished set reveal from an angle that clearly shows the art complexity.
  7. Optional talking-head line: "Send the inspo first, and I will help you choose the right service."

Editing Direction

  • Use a simple progression: simple, classic art, more detailed.
  • Overlay text: "Price depends on service, length, and design."
  • Avoid exact bundled or new-client offer claims until pricing is approved.
  • End text: "Send inspo for the right appointment."

Production Copy

On-Screen Text
  • What does a custom set cost?
  • Service + length + design.
  • Send inspo for the right appointment.
Caption

The easiest way to know what to book is to send the inspo. I will help you match the design to the right service, length, and art tier.

Optional VO

Custom set pricing depends on the service, the length, and how detailed the design is. Send the inspo first and I will point you to the right appointment.

Finished Video Should Look Like

A visual-first 9-13 second Reel that feels like "I have somewhere to be, and my nails need to look good in every photo." The nails should look finished, intentional, and camera-ready. This is not a separate vacation offer; it routes people back into the main custom set or first set path.

Shot List

  1. Opening shot of the finished set with the hand holding a bag, cup, keys, or sunglasses if available.
  2. Macro shot of the most photo-worthy detail.
  3. Slow hand movement in natural light, like the client is checking the nails.
  4. Desk or process shot showing one detail being added.
  5. Final reveal with both hands together.
  6. Optional lifestyle shot: hand on steering wheel, phone, purse, or outfit detail. Keep it clean and not too busy.
  7. Optional Brenda voiceover line: "If you have a trip, birthday, photos, or an event coming up, send the inspo early."

Editing Direction

  • Make the first frame the most scroll-stopping finished set.
  • Use faster cuts than the education videos.
  • Overlay text: "Trip? Birthday? Photos? Book the set first."
  • End text: "DM SET" or "Send your inspo."

Day 6

Private Suite Stories

HookPrivate suite, one artist, clean prep.
Format5-7 Instagram Story frames.
CTAPoll: Need a new set?

Finished Story Should Look Like

A calm walk-through of what it feels like to come to Beauty Hive. The viewer should see that this is a private appointment, not a crowded salon. It should build trust: clean space, organized tools, Brenda's desk, the booking path, and a simple poll or question box.

Story Frame List

  1. Exterior or entry shot. Text: "Private nail studio near SDSU"
  2. Wide studio shot with chair, desk, lamp, setup. Text: "One-on-one appointments"
  3. Clean prep/tool shot. Text: "Clean prep. No rushed sets."
  4. Brenda working with a client hand. Text: "Bring your inspo"
  5. Finished nail close-up. Text: "Custom sets + structured nails"
  6. Booking screen or link-in-bio screen recording. Text: "Book through the link in bio"
  7. Poll sticker: "Need a new set?" Options: "Yes" and "Send inspo"

Filming Direction

  • Move slowly. No fast walking shots.
  • Keep each Story clip under 5 seconds.
  • Use the same filter or color temperature across all frames.
  • Do not overfill with text. One short line per Story is enough.

Sticker / Caption
