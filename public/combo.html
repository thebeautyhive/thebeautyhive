What to Expect

Your appointment, start to finish.

No surprises, no rushing. Here's exactly how the next two-plus hours will go.

1

Arrive & settle in

Park in our dedicated spaces and walk into a quiet, private, plant-filled studio. No front-desk wait — Brenda is already set up for you. Sip something warm while you decide on your design.

2

1-on-1 consultation

Brenda walks you through the three nail systems (Gel-X, Acrylic, Builder Gel), looks at your natural nails, and helps you choose what'll wear best for your lifestyle. Bring inspo photos — she loves them.

3

Service begins — hands first

Cuticle prep, shaping, application, and the start of your custom art. The botanical hand massage and paraffin wax are woven through — not rushed, not bolted on at the end.

4

Feet, slowly

Soak, prep, and a full set on toes that matches or complements your hands. The hot towel wrap finishes the foot service. Most guests describe this stretch as "the best nap I never took."

5

Finishing touches

Final art passes, top coat, and a side-by-side review under the studio lights to make sure every nail is exactly where you want it. If anything's even slightly off, Brenda fixes it before you leave.

6

Aftercare & rebooking

You'll leave with simple aftercare guidance to keep your set looking fresh and the option to lock in your next appointment before you walk out — most guests rebook on the spot.

Before You Book

Quick answers, no surprises.

Which Memorial Day tier is right for me?
The $149 Set Special is the entry tier — great for students, first-time clients, or anyone who just wants a clean summer set. The $159 Private Set + Pedi is the headline tier and the most popular pick — full set plus express pedi so hands and toes are sandal-ready. The $179 VIP Studio Experience is the full afternoon: premium pedi, removal if needed, beverage, full custom art allowance, and priority rebooking. Every tier is a private 1-on-1 appointment.
How long does the appointment take?
Plan for about 1.5–2 hours for the $149 Set Special, 2–2.5 hours for the $159 Private Set + Pedi, and 2.5–3 hours for the $179 VIP Studio Experience. The whole point is that you're not rushed, so we won't squeeze you out the door.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
A $25 deposit secures your appointment. Reschedule or cancel with at least 48 hours' notice and your deposit transfers to a future visit — no questions asked. Within 48 hours, the deposit is forfeited so we can offer the spot to another guest. Life happens; just give us a heads-up.
Do you take walk-ins?
No — by appointment only. The studio is set up for one guest at a time, and Brenda only takes 3–4 clients per day so each appointment gets the full undistracted experience. Booking online (or via DM) is the only way in.
Is the Memorial Day pricing only for that weekend?
The Summer Ready Collection runs through Memorial Day weekend and the surrounding week. Because Brenda only takes 3–4 clients per day, weekend slots fill first — weekday appointments at the same Memorial Day pricing are typically still available a little later. Booked-out? Join the summer list above and you'll get first access to next openings.
What products do you use?
Premium professional-grade products only — soft-gel extensions, builder gel, and salon-grade acrylic systems. Paraffin and massage products are botanical and skin-safe. All implements are sterilized between guests; pumice and disposable items are single-use.
What's the difference between Acrylic, Gel-X, and Builder Gel?
Acrylic is the most durable, especially for long lengths. Gel-X uses pre-shaped soft-gel tips for a lighter, more natural feel. Builder Gel is the gentlest on natural nails and ideal for adding strength without much length. Brenda will help you pick the right one when you arrive.
Where exactly are you located?
Beauty Hive Studio is at 6244 El Cajon Blvd, Suite 21, San Diego, CA 92115 — in the College Area, just minutes from SDSU. We have dedicated parking spaces. Full directions are in the Find Us section below.
