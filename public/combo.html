Before You Book

Quick answers, no surprises.

Is it really worth $180?
Honest answer: if you want a quick polish change, no — book a regular manicure. The Hands & Feet Combo is for the woman who wants her one beauty appointment of the month to actually feel like a luxury. You're paying for a 2+ hour private session, premium products, hand-painted custom art, paraffin wax, hot towel wrap, and a hand massage — done by one nail artist who only takes a handful of clients per day. The price reflects what you're getting and what you're not getting (rushed work, salon noise, an assembly line).
How long does the appointment take?
Plan for about 2 hours 15 minutes for the Short/Medium combo and 2.5–3 hours for Long/Extra-Long. The whole point is that you're not rushed, so we won't squeeze you out the door.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
A $25 deposit secures your appointment. Reschedule or cancel with at least 48 hours' notice and your deposit transfers to a future visit — no questions asked. Within 48 hours, the deposit is forfeited so we can offer the spot to another guest. Life happens; just give us a heads-up.
Do you take walk-ins?
No — by appointment only. The studio is set up for one guest at a time, and Brenda only takes 3–4 clients per day so each appointment gets the full undistracted experience. Booking online (or via DM) is the only way in.
What products do you use?
Premium professional-grade products only — soft-gel extensions, builder gel, and salon-grade acrylic systems. Paraffin and massage products are botanical and skin-safe. All implements are sterilized between guests; pumice and disposable items are single-use.
What's the difference between Acrylic, Gel-X, and Builder Gel?
Acrylic is the most durable, especially for long lengths. Gel-X uses pre-shaped soft-gel tips for a lighter, more natural feel. Builder Gel is the gentlest on natural nails and ideal for adding strength without much length. Brenda will help you pick the right one when you arrive.
Where exactly are you located?
Beauty Hive Studio is at 6244 El Cajon Blvd, Suite 21, San Diego, CA 92115 — in the College Area, just minutes from SDSU. We have dedicated parking spaces. Full directions are in the Find Us section below.
