# Brenda 4-Week Shot List

Purpose: keep the detailed shot direction for planned videos. This is a
production reference, not the thing to send Brenda while she is actively
shooting.

For shoot-day check-ins, use
`marketing/brenda-next-video-capture-brief.md`. Send Brenda only the next video,
the must-have shots, one optional talking line, and the CTA.

Every video should be vertical 9:16, bright, steady, and close enough that the
nails are the main character.

General filming rules:

- Film every clip vertically.
- Hold each shot for 4-6 seconds, even if the final edit uses only 1 second.
- Wipe the phone camera before filming.
- Use natural window light or the nail desk light. Avoid yellow room lighting.
- Film both a wide version and a close version whenever possible.
- Keep backgrounds clean: table wiped, tools lined up, no clutter, no trash.
- Capture natural sound. The final edit can use music or voiceover, but clean
  real audio makes the video feel more premium.
- For every finished set, capture: thumb angle, flat palm, curled hand, slow
  hand turn, and one macro detail shot.

Reuse rule for Week 1:

If an existing Reel is reused, treat it as a remix, not a duplicate repost. Pick
one job for the reused clip before editing: proof, education, personality, or
booking. Then change the first 1-2 seconds, the text overlay, the caption angle,
and the CTA so the audience gets a new reason to watch.

Quick reuse checklist:

1. Best first frame: finished nails, Brenda's face, or the process moment that
   makes the post stop the scroll.
2. One message only: what the viewer should understand before booking.
3. One CTA only: `SET`, `GROW`, `DESIGN`, `BOOK`, or link in bio.
4. Trim repeated context. Reused Reels should usually be tighter than the
   original.
5. If the reused asset is only a still or weak clip, use it as a carousel/static
   or send it to motion adaptation instead of forcing a Reel.

## Day 1 - Sculpted Set / First Set With Art

Calendar hook: "San Diego, your first set should come with art."

Use the Growth Plan's Day 1 reference: `DYC74flpHYB`. Repost or remake that
nail-talk style post with a clearer `DM SET` CTA. If there is no usable video,
film a finished set with clean art detail.

Finished video should look like:

A polished 10-14 second Reel that opens with the finished nails immediately.
The first frame should be a tight, bright close-up of the full set, not Brenda
talking and not a title screen. It should feel like someone scrolling Instagram
stops because the art is detailed, clean, and wearable. The edit should move
from final result, to detail, to Brenda's hands working, then back to the final
reveal. The tone is premium but friendly: "this is your first full set with
Brenda, and she will help you choose the right appointment."

Shot list:

1. Tight opening shot of the finished set, fingers slightly curved, nails filling
   most of the frame.
2. Slow side-to-side hand turn so the shine, shape, and art catch the light.
3. Macro shot of the best art detail, held steady.
4. Brenda brushing top coat, chrome, detail paint, or placing a small art detail.
5. Wide desk shot showing Brenda working with the client hand in frame.
6. Final reveal with the client's hand resting on a clean surface or near a
   simple prop, no busy background.
7. Optional 2-second Brenda clip smiling or saying, "If you are new, send me
   your inspo."

Editing direction:

- Start with the prettiest finished-nail shot.
- Cut every 1-2 seconds.
- Keep text minimal: "Your first set should come with art."
- End text: "DM SET" or "Book your first set."
- Do not over-explain the offer until Brenda approves final terms.

Production copy:

On-screen text:

- "San Diego, your first set should come with art."
- "Bring the inspo you have been saving."
- "DM SET to book with Brenda."

"San Diego, if you have been saving nail inspo, this is your sign to book the
