# Brenda Weeks 2-4 Content Queue

Purpose: extend the first seven-day Beauty Hive content queue into weeks 2
through 4, with post-ready hooks, CTAs, production notes, and paid creative
candidates.

This file is the queue. For the moment Brenda checks in while filming, use
`marketing/brenda-next-video-capture-brief.md` and give her one video objective
at a time.

Operating rule: keep the public campaign anchored to one clear front-end path:
new clients DM `SET`, `GROW`, `DESIGN`, or `BOOK` depending on intent. Do not
publish exact new-client offer terms, bundled totals, scarcity, or classic-art
claims until Brenda/OU approve the offer and Acuity booking mechanic.

Goal: answer the reasons people hesitate before booking: lifting, inspo
uncertainty, service choice, art timing, natural nail protection, pricing, and
private-suite trust.

## Batch Shoot Request For Weeks 2-4

Brenda can cover the whole three-week queue with one 90-minute batch:

- Prep: cuticle work, shaping, dehydrator/primer, base/structure.
- Detail: art brush, French line, chrome, 3D charm, top coat.
- Reveals: flat palm, curled hand, side angle, slow hand turn, macro art.
- Studio: door/sign, chair, plants, clean tools, one-on-one desk setup.
- Brenda: five 8-12 second talking-head answers:
  - "Why I do not rush a structured set."
  - "What makes a set lift early."
  - "How I price design sets from inspo."
  - "What to book if you are new."
  - "Why Beauty Hive is private appointment-only."
