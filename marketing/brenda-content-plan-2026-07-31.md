# Brenda Content Plan

Objective: create a repeatable content system that turns Brenda's existing nail
work and personality into booked appointments, qualified DMs, and a clear
creative testing pipeline for Meta.

This file is the strategy and calendar. It is not the shoot-day checklist.
When Brenda is filming and asks what to capture next, use
`marketing/brenda-next-video-capture-brief.md` first.

Offer discipline: keep the launch to one clear front-end offer, with one
optional secondary offer only if Brenda/OU choose to test it. Vacation, birthday,
bridal, concert, and event language are content angles for the same first-set
offer, not separate public offers or DM keywords.

## Strategy

Brenda should not post like a generic salon. The strongest position is:

> San Diego private-suite nail artist for structured, long-lasting, design-forward nails.

The account needs two signals at the same time:

- Art-led proof: the nail work is detailed enough to stop the scroll.
- Owner-led trust: people care about Brenda's personality, studio story, and
  how she thinks about the appointment.

Current Instagram data supports this. Brenda's biggest reach outlier is a nail
art/process Reel, while high-comment posts come from personal and studio-story
content. Do not sterilize the voice.

## Reel Reuse Direction

Do not reuse a Reel by posting the same asset with a slightly different caption.
Every reused Reel needs a new job in the funnel, a new first-frame hook, and a
clearer booking action than the original post.

Reuse treatments:

| Treatment | When to use | What changes | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Proof cut` | The nails are already visually strong. | Open on the best finished-nail frame, trim to 8-12 seconds, add one short overlay naming the result. | DM `SET` or `DESIGN` |
| `Education cut` | The clip shows prep, structure, art, or grow-out. | Reorder the clip around one lesson: problem, process, result. Add 2-3 labels only. | DM `GROW`, `SET`, or `DESIGN` |
| `Personality cut` | Brenda's voice, humor, or studio energy is the reason people cared. | Keep the human moment, then cut quickly back to nails so the service still sells. | Book link or DM `SET` |
| `Booking cut` | A warm audience has already seen the art/proof. | Add a stronger end card: what to send, what to book, or which keyword to DM. | DM `SET`, `GROW`, or `BOOK` |

Reuse rules:

- Change the opening 1-2 seconds first. The first frame should never feel like a
  duplicate if someone already saw the original.
- Keep the same creative asset, but change the reason it exists: proof,
  education, personality, or booking.
- Preserve Brenda's natural voice. Do not make reused Reels sound like generic
  salon ads.
- Use one CTA per post. Occasion, vacation, birthday, and photo content still
  routes to `SET`, not a separate keyword.
- Send strong static nail frames to motion adaptation only when the still has
  enough detail to stop the scroll and can end with a booking CTA.

## New Content Brenda Needs To Create

Use `marketing/brenda-next-video-capture-brief.md` for the active shoot request.
The list below is the broader monthly inventory, not what Brenda needs to read
when she is standing there with a client.

One 90-minute batch shoot can cover the next month if she has time, but a normal
check-in should ask for only one video at a time.

### Must-Have Clips

- Prep: cuticle work, shaping, dehydrator/primer, structure/base.
- Detail: art brush, 3D charms, chrome, French line, top coat.
- Reveal: final hand turn, before/after hand entry, client hand pose.
- Studio: door/sign, chair, tools, plants, private-suite wide shot.
- Brenda: 5 short talking-head clips answering common questions.
- Booking: phone screen scrolling booking page or DM/inspo handoff.

### Talking-Head Prompts

- "Why I do not rush a structured set."
- "What makes a set lift early."
- "How I price design sets from inspo."
- "What to book if you are new."
- "Why Beauty Hive is private appointment-only."

### Shot Rules

- Film vertical 9:16.
- Hold every shot for at least 4 seconds.
- Get both clean wide light and tight macro detail.
- Capture natural audio, even if voiceover is added later.
- Shoot the same set in three stages: prep, art, final reveal.

## AI-Generated Content Track

Use AI to extend Brenda's real content, not replace her identity.

Allowed:

- Nail-art scene extensions from Brenda-owned stills.
- Background/studio mood images that do not fake a client testimonial.
- Product/process explainer visuals.
- Motion versions of real nail stills.
- Hook variants, captions, overlays, and storyboards.

Not allowed:

- Fake Brenda likeness.
- Fake client reviews.
- Fake before/after claims.
- AI hands that misrepresent the actual service result in BOF ads.

### Scale AI / Skool-Informed Process

Use the community/research process as the front end:

1. Pull niche patterns from Skool/community lessons and beauty-account examples:
   hooks, objections, proof formats, offer mechanics, and DM CTAs.
2. Convert each pattern into a Beauty Hive-specific creative brief:
   visual hook, undeniable proof, theme, funnel stage, CTA, and required asset.
3. Map the brief to either existing Brenda assets, a new shoot request, or AI generation.
4. Generate only the missing pieces: scene frames, transitions, product/process explainers,
   and image-to-video motion.
5. Put every generated output into an approval queue before posting or launching.
6. After Meta launch, score by DM cost, qualified lead cost, booked appointments,
   and AOV, not by views alone.

## Production Workflow

1. Weekly: Content Agent selects 10 briefs from this plan.
2. Brenda or OU marks each as `reuse`, `shoot`, or `generate`.
3. Reuse assets are edited first.
4. Shoot requests are batched into one filming list for Brenda.
5. Generated assets use the Eskiin fal route, with Beauty Hive outputs saved in
   the Beauty Hive/Hermes project paths only.
6. Approved assets move into Meta testing.
