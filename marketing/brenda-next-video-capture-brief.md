# Brenda Next Video Capture Brief

Purpose: this is the shoot-day doc. It is not the strategy, calendar, ad plan,
or caption bank. When Brenda checks in before or during filming, answer from
this file first and give her only what she needs to capture the next video.

## How To Use This

When Brenda asks what to shoot, pick one video objective and send back:

1. The next video name.
2. The 5-7 must-have shots.
3. One optional talking line.
4. The CTA.

Do not send the whole content plan unless she asks for it.

## Current Next Video

Video: `First Set / Send The Inspo`

Goal: get a potential new client to DM `SET` with nail inspo or click the
booking link.

Hook: `San Diego, send the nail inspo you have been saving.`

CTA: `DM SET`

Must-capture shots:

1. Finished set close-up, nails filling most of the frame.
2. Slow hand turn so the shape and shine catch the light.
3. Macro shot of the best art detail.
4. Brenda doing one process step: top coat, detail paint, chrome, charm, or file.
5. Client hand resting on a clean surface for the final reveal.
6. Quick phone shot of an inspo photo or Brenda looking at inspo.
7. Optional Brenda clip: "Send me your inspo and I will help you choose what to book."

Editing note:

- Open with the finished nails, not a title screen.
- Keep the video 8-14 seconds.
- Use one message only: send inspo, Brenda helps you book the right set.

## If She Is Filming A Client Right Now

Ask for these in this order:

1. Before or blank canvas shot.
2. Prep/cuticle/shaping shot.
3. One detailed process shot.
4. Finished set close-up.
5. Slow hand turn.
6. Macro art detail.
7. Brenda or client moment, only if natural.

If she can only capture three things, get:

1. Finished close-up.
2. Process detail.
3. Final hand turn.

## Capture Rules

- Film vertical 9:16.
- Hold each shot for 4-6 seconds.
- Wipe the phone camera.
- Use window light or the nail desk light.
- Keep the table clean.
- Get close enough that the nails are obvious.
- Capture natural audio even if the final edit uses music.
